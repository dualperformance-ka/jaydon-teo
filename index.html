01 / THE PROBLEMYou are not the problem

Three gym sessions, three runs, 21:30 for the 5k, a program you already follow. The effort is there. The coordination is not.

You do not have to choose between running faster, getting stronger and getting through GAMSAT. You need one plan that controls training stress, nutrition, recovery and time.

02 / THE PLAN13 weeks

Week 0
Discovery & baseline

Benchmark run, key lifts, body weight, food and protein snapshot, sleep, knee and ankle history. Nothing prescribed until we see real numbers.

Weeks 1 to 4
Build the system

Gym and run programming locked together. Nutrition targets set from your baseline.

Weeks 5 to 9
Push

Running intensity climbs where recovery allows. Lifts get an explicit progression rule.

Weeks 10 to 12
Consolidate & hand over

Retest, then a plan you run on your own.

Nutrition phase is chosen after discovery, not before. Maintenance, gradual recomposition or a controlled lean gain, depending on your baseline. We will tell you which, and why.
